# Kintsu Shop

- Runs at http://localhost:3000. No login.
- One product: Kintsu Mug, $40.00, at /product/mug.
- Cart at /cart: quantity, a coupon code field with an Apply coupon button, and an Add gift wrap ($5.00) checkbox.
- Checkout at /checkout shows Merchandise, Discount, Gift wrap, Tax and Total, and a Pay button.
- Confirmation at /confirmation shows Total paid.
- Money is shown in dollars with two decimals.
